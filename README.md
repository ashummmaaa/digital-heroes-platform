# Digital Heroes

Digital Heroes is a subscription-based golf performance and charitable impact platform that transforms regular golf participation into measurable social impact.

Subscribers can track their Stableford golf scores, select a charity, contribute a percentage of their subscription fee, and participate in monthly prize draws.

## Project Overview

Digital Heroes combines three core experiences:

1. **Play** — Subscribers record and manage their golf Stableford scores.
2. **Support** — Subscribers select a charity and allocate a percentage of their subscription.
3. **Win** — Eligible subscribers participate in monthly prize draws.

The platform includes separate experiences for public visitors, subscribers, and administrators.
